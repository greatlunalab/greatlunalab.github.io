Work with Us

We're looking for curious, motivated students and researchers to join the LUNA Lab.

We are actively looking for new students, including new PhD students for Fall 2027, especially those interested in generative UI, UI agents, eye tracking, digital accessibility, AR/VR, or related areas.

Our group works on technical HCI: training AI models that simulate user behavior and building adaptive systems for human-centered problems. Our projects are primarily computational, centered on developing models and systems end to end and evaluating them against human data.

Research Areas We're Excited About

  • Generative UI
  • UI Agents & Automation
  • Eye Tracking & Attention
  • Digital Accessibility
  • AR / VR & Immersive Systems
  • Computer Vision & Multimodal AI
  • User Behavior Simulation
  • Adaptive Interfaces

Prospective PhD Students

We are recruiting PhD students for Fall 2027 through the School of Computing at the University of Utah. If you're excited about generative UI, UI agents, eye tracking, digital accessibility, or AR/VR, please apply and mention Prof. Yue Jiang in your application.

Preferred qualifications:

  • Experience in AR/VR (Unity, Unreal, immersive systems) or computer vision / AI-ML modeling (vision, multimodal, reinforcement learning)
  • HCI research experience with quantitative or systems contributions
  • Solid programming skills: Python and PyTorch, or C#/C++ for AR/VR

Candidates with a strong technical background who are new to HCI are also encouraged to apply.
